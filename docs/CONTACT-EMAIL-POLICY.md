# Optional developer contact email policy

Contact email remains optional. Validation applies to new submissions, owner editing and every database contact write. It checks a practical ASCII dot-atom mailbox, DNS labels, length limits, a configured public suffix and known disposable providers (including subdomains). It does not prove DNS/MX availability, ownership or an inbox's existence. IDN domains may use their ASCII/punycode form; country-code suffixes are supported.

`lib/contact-email-policy.json` is the reviewed configuration shared by browser validation and the disposable-provider helper. Its allowlist uses the versioned [IANA root-zone suffix snapshot](https://data.iana.org/TLD/tlds-alpha-by-domain.txt) recorded in that file, excluding the infrastructure-only `.arpa` suffix. This deliberately includes common suffixes (`.com`, `.org`, `.net`, `.uk`, `.io`, `.dev`), international/country-code suffixes, and legitimate indie-developer suffixes such as `.games`, `.studio` and `.gg`. A domain ending `.co.uk` is covered by `.uk`. A business need not use a familiar mail provider. Non-public/test suffixes and invented suffixes such as `.cock` and `.coma` are rejected by this product policy even when a string passes syntax checks.

The database configuration lives in private `public.contact_email_policy`. Migration 015 seeds the exact same suffix/provider arrays; `tests/test-submission-contacts.mjs` checks their equality and frontend/database parity. Updating policy requires a reviewed JSON change and a new ordered migration updating that singleton, followed by deployment of the matching application. Do not change only an environment variable, frontend list or database row. The current blocklist is finite and is not a promise to identify all disposable providers. Review it periodically rather than blocking domains solely because they are unfamiliar.

Historical stored contact/evidence values are preserved. The contact check is NOT VALID for historical rows, while future inserts/updates enforce the current policy. Owners can correct or clear a historical value. No contact field or evidence/AI-disclosure column is removed. The general submission and correction forms hide supporting evidence; claim evidence remains available. Existing evidence still participates in the existing disclosure/verification model.
