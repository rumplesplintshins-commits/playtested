# Terms, Privacy and Cookie wording review

4 November 2026; implementation reviewed at `94939b9`. **Review complete; final wording/operational/legal approval remains open.** This pass records findings and proposed wording only; it does not edit the public application, set an effective date, record user acceptance or certify compliance.

The public Terms and Privacy pages render `lib/site-information.ts` through `SiteInformation.tsx`. Cookie/browser-storage information is inside the Privacy page and the footer control; there is no separate final Cookie Policy. Both pages explicitly remain drafts with no effective date. The draft identifies the individual operator trading as Playtested, but expressly leaves the service address, jurisdiction, lawful-basis assessments, provider arrangements and deletion review open. Do not publish it as a completed launch notice merely because development functionality passed.

## Wording/operational items requiring approval before production GO

1. **Final operator details and effective notice:** confirm service/contact address, applicable jurisdiction, notice/effective date, material-change/terms-acceptance handling and an appropriate rights/complaints route. The app currently says it does not record acceptance; this must not be represented as implemented consent to final Terms. The cookie section's embedded “Last updated: 2 November” also needs a consistent approved review/effective date when updated.
2. **Lawful-basis and provider review:** approve actual purposes and basis for service/admin/security, optional outreach, recruitment/self-reporting and analytics; document any assessments. Verify Supabase/Render/Google/video-provider regions, agreements and transfers against real account settings. Source code cannot establish those arrangements. No paid service or account-configuration change was made for this review.
3. **Deletion and retention operation:** approve the fulfilment process above and a realistic retention schedule. The generic 12-month moderation/support and 90-day historical-journey/log wording is a proposed policy, not a verified automated purge. Confirm who executes deletion, handles failed screenshot cleanup/retention jobs and removes aged backup copies. If no workable process can be approved, this is a release blocker requiring separately authorised remediation—not a wording-only waiver.
4. **Complete browser-storage disclosure:** add the two existing functional entries below, which are missing from the current detailed storage list. Classifying an entry as functional/necessary and whether any consent exception applies is for the owner/legal review; the code-level observation alone does not establish the legal conclusion.
5. **Media-provider choice:** approve the actual Play disclosure and withdrawal/close behaviour. A decision to add a different consent mechanism, provider integration or autoplay would require separately authorised application work; none was introduced in this gate pass.
6. **Accurate evidence language:** approve the distinction between legacy public evidence and private ownership-claim evidence. The underlying visibility must remain unchanged.

## Proposed wording for owner/legal review — not published

**Additional functional browser storage:** “When you open a game from Tester Hub, sessionStorage can remember its campaign reference, expanded cards, genre choice and return position for up to 30 minutes, checked when you return. Entries are removed when consumed or expired and normally disappear when the tab session ends. Clear read in the notification bell saves a local timestamp (`playtested-bell-cleared-at-v2`) to hide older read alerts from that popup. That timestamp has no automatic expiry in the current implementation and remains until replaced or cleared with browser site data; it does not delete server notifications.” Browser session restore may preserve tab storage. The timestamp key is origin-wide, not account-scoped; do not claim it contains account/message contents or is erased on logout.

**Evidence clarification:** “The general Submit a Game form no longer asks for supporting-evidence links. Evidence links on older approved listings and published assessment summaries may remain public. Evidence supplied for a developer ownership claim is handled in the claim review workflow and is not automatically made public on your game listing.”

**Notification record clarification:** “Playtested records service notifications, delivery/read information and event keys to send the right account relevant updates and prevent duplicate delivery. Dismissing an alert does not necessarily erase the underlying delivery key. These records are handled as part of account-deletion fulfilment and the adopted service-retention policy.” The operator must approve concrete retention periods/criteria rather than treating this proposed sentence as a substitute for them.
