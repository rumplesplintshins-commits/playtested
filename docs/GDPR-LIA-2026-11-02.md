# Playtested legitimate interests assessment (LIA)

Reviewed: 2 November 2026

This internal assessment supports the limited Playtested processing currently described as relying on legitimate interests. It is not a blanket basis for all processing.

## Interests

Playtested has legitimate interests in keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and spam, enforcing tester/developer eligibility and campaign rules, moderating reports and ownership claims, investigating disputes, and maintaining a reliable audit trail where proportionate.

## Necessity

These purposes require limited account identifiers, event timestamps, moderation reasons, security/rate-limit information and relevant service records. Less intrusive alternatives should be used where they achieve the same purpose. Public advertising profiling and unrelated behavioural tracking are not necessary for these interests and are not part of this assessment.

## Balancing

Users reasonably expect a game-testing service to protect accounts, prevent duplicate/abusive participation, moderate feedback, investigate ownership and quality disputes, and retain a limited record of those actions. The main privacy risks are excessive retention, unnecessary linkage of historic feedback to an identifiable account, and using service/security records for unrelated purposes.

Safeguards are therefore: role-based/RLS access; purpose limitation; no advertising pixels or third-party analytics SDKs in the reviewed application; disabled new shared-link journey attribution; defined retention targets; de-identification of useful historic feedback where appropriate after account closure; user access/correction/deletion/objection routes; and admin reasons/audit records for moderation actions.

## Retention targets

Routine historic shared-link attribution and ordinary technical/security logs: normally up to 90 days unless required for an active security/legal matter. Resolved moderation/security records and resolved support correspondence: normally up to 12 months where still justified. Developer browser-only feedback follow-up choices: up to 180 days after the last change. Unsubmitted Tester Hub feedback drafts: target 30 days after the last edit once the expiry change is deployed. Provider backups follow the relevant provider lifecycle.

## Outcome

The interests above may be relied on where the processing remains necessary and proportionate under these safeguards. Reassess if Playtested adds advertising, analytics/profiling, payments, substantially different moderation, new categories of personal data, or materially different sharing/retention.
